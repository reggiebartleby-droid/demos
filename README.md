# Jump Duck Faster

A mobile reflex game: race three rival ducks up a spiral water slide.
Hop the yellow hurdles, duck under the red bars, and don't get washed into the pool.

**Play:** open the GitHub Pages link for this repo on your phone or computer.

## Controls
- **Phone:** hold the red **Duck** pad (bottom left), tap or hold the yellow **Jump** pad (bottom right).
- **Keyboard:** Up / W / Space to jump, Down / S to duck, P or Esc to pause.
- **Y:** toggle invincibility for your duck (for testing; shows "Invincible (Y)" under your place).

## Notes
- One self-contained file (`index.html`). It loads three.js r128 from cdnjs and fonts from Google Fonts,
  so it needs an internet connection.
- Your level unlocks are saved in your own browser (localStorage), per device.
- Build: v24 (2026-10-04).
