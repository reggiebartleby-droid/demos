The hex map won. Now try Echoes.

The hex map now has two new things to test: Friend Echoes and torchlight. Fight to fill the Echo meter, then call in a guildmate (or a wandering hero) for two actions. Spend both at once, or save one for next turn. The older square samples are still below for reference. The art is placeholder: battle cards will get detailed portraits later.

  • 1 Tap one of your crew
  • 2 Tap a lit space to move
  • 3 Tap a ghost marked red to attack

Palms, rocks and torches block movement. Ghosts float, so water never stops them. Try both grid settings too.
