Project Fishnet · design summary · October 2026

Project Fishnet

A funny, loot-hungry, turn-based RPG on a tropical island at night, where the parties are loud, the ghosts are louder, and something under the sand is very, very old.

Turn-based RPG Hex battles Phone, tablet and PC (browser) Solo battles, shared guild Loot first
01 · The island

Funny on the surface. Spooky underneath.

One big tropical island after dark: festival lights, music drifting from the resorts, and ghosts, secrets and strange lights everywhere else. Long ago something fell from the stars. The island hasn't been normal since. A big overworld opens up region by region as the story moves.

The Five Lanterns

The island's factions each fly their own lantern color over their territory. Look up at the lights and you know whose turf you're on.

The Amber Lantern

Adventurers and explorers

Want to map every inch of the island and every secret under it.

The Silver Lantern

Researchers and stargazers

Want to understand the island's strange lights and the thing that fell.

The Copper Lantern

Engineers and tinkerers

Want to bottle the island's power and put it to work.

The Rose Lantern

The wealthy, who run the nightlife

Own the resorts and the best parties, and want to own everything else.

The Hollow Lantern

A secret cult

A lantern that gives no light. Its members hide inside the other four. Nobody knows what they want.

02 · Your hero

Pick a race, a class and an aura

Everyone builds one hero, then recruits companions: people from the Lanterns and creatures from around the island. As you level up, your aura unlocks hybrid abilities and your hero's look changes with their powers.

Races

Classic

Islanders

Regular humans. The place to start.

Mysterious

Starfallen

Descended from whatever crashed here. Faint starlight under the skin.

Strange

Oddlings

Mostly human, with one thing off: a horn, glowing freckles, too many shadows.

Talkative

Maskborn

A living mask that shares your face and has opinions about your choices. Loudly.

Haunted

Hollowed

Touched by the Void. They can see and talk to ghosts.

Classes

Eight classes with clear jobs and real trade-offs, built from everyone's ideas.

Melee damage

Duelist

Fast, slippery and deadly up close. Big critical hits.

Tank

Guardian

Shields the crew and carves protective runes onto hexes.

Ranged damage

Ranger

Bows, crossbows and thrown weapons. Hex-cursed arrows.

Utility · loot

Rogue

Finds bonus loot, sets traps, steals enemy buffs.

Healer · curses

Shaman

Keeps the crew alive and makes enemies regret things.

Support

Seer

Reads the stars to boost allies and see enemy moves coming.

Area control

Tide Mage

Floods hexes, pushes enemies around, bends the battlefield.

Pet class

Beastmaster

Fights side by side with a tamed island creature.

Auras

Your aura shapes how your powers look and behave, and what loot drops for you. No aura beats another.

Moon · healing and protection Void · stealth and draining Flame · bursts of damage Tide · movement and control Storm · chain lightning Star · rare and mysterious

03 · Battles

Hexes, torchlight and a little help from your friends

The battle map from the playable sample. Rocks, palms, torches and the lagoon block your crew. Ghosts float right over the water.

  • Hex grid over a painted mapTilted halfway between top-down and isometric, so it looks rich and still taps easily on a phone.
  • Quick, solo fightsYour hero plus companions on a small board, a few minutes per battle.
  • See what's comingTap an enemy to light up, in red, everywhere it can move next turn.
  • Torchlit nightsFlickering torches, rising embers and festival lanterns set the mood.

Friend Echoes

You fight alone, but you're never really alone. Fill the Echo meter during a fight, then call in a guildmate's echo for two actions.

  1. Attacks, moves and hits fill the meter.
  2. Pick a friend's echo, or a wandering hero if nobody's around.
  3. Spend both actions at once, or save one for next turn.
  4. Your friend gets a thank-you next time they log in.

Echoes scale to the fight, not to your friend, so they stay useful all game and never become pay-to-win.

04 · Loot

Every good drop should feel like a party

Five rarity tiers, each with its own color, glow and pop when it drops. Think Borderlands 2 and Destiny. Tier names are still up for grabs.

Tier 1Random stats. The stuff you swap out fast.
Tier 2Random stats, a little better.
Tier 3Random stats, worth a second look.
Tier 4Random stats, build-defining rolls.
LegendaryFixed base stats, one signature ability, plus a chance of 1–3 bonus abilities.
Upgrading

Feed it

Power up gear you love by feeding it gear you don't. No collecting piles of materials first.

Fair drops

Same rarity for everyone

In guild fights everyone gets their own loot, at the same rarity.

Best loot

Go big

Main bosses, raids and rare quests drop the best gear. Your class and aura steer what drops.

Some weapons are jokes, and you won't know until you swing them. That gleaming fire wand? It's a hair dryer. The fearsome slingshot fires harmless gumballs.

05 · Playing together

Together, even when you're not online at the same time

Guild

Shared quests

Work toward guild goals and share resources.

Guild bosses

One boss, everyone's damage

A huge shared health bar. Everyone fights it in their own battles, and it all adds up.

PvP

Fight your friends' heroes

Battle a friend's saved hero, played by the computer with their real gear.

The long game

It doesn't really end

A big twist, a final boss, then New Game+, events, bosses and raids.

06 · House rules

Things this game will never do

✕

Pay-to-win.

✕

Grinding piles of materials before you can upgrade.

✕

Rock-paper-scissors counters.

✕

Crafting, survival or roguelike loops.

✕

Forcing you into a guild. Solo players always have wandering heroes.
