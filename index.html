Jump Duck Faster

Race three rival ducks up the water slide to the finish arch. Every level speeds up as you go.

  • Yellow hurdles: hop. Quick tap on Jump (bottom right), or tap ↑ / Space.
  • Red bars: duck. Hold Duck (bottom left) or ↓; you stay down while you hold. You can press one while holding the other.
  • Each miss and a wave washes you back down the slide. Run out of stumbles and you're in the pool.

Enter a name first, so your runs show up under it.

Levels

Every tenth level is a boss. Beat it to unlock the next chapter.
