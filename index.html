    Things to know

    • Neither Chrono Trigger nor Octopath lets you move. In Chrono Trigger the fight happens wherever you were standing, and the game moves everyone around; where they land decides who an area attack hits. Octopath has no positions at all. The only one of the three with real player movement is the Baldur's Gate 3 style, Free step.
    • What the hex grid gives you for free: ranges you can count ("3 hexes"), taps that snap to a spot (easy on a phone), simple pathfinding around rocks and water, and simple enemy AI. Free step has to rebuild all of that with circles, collision checks and a real pathfinder. This mockup cheats and only walks in straight lines.
    • A middle path: movement that looks free but secretly snaps to a hidden hex grid. Players see no lines; the code still counts hexes. Stage mode could also run on hidden hexes, so area attacks stay predictable.
    • On a phone, tap a ghost once to preview, tap it again to confirm. In Free step, tap the ground once to see the path, tap the same spot again to walk. That double tap is the price of free aiming on touch screens.
