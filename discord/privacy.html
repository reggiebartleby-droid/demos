Privacy Policy

Jump Duck Faster (Discord Activity and web game)
Effective date: October 11, 2026

Jump Duck Faster ("the game") is a small game made and run by Reginald Bartleby ("I", "me"). This page explains what information the game uses, why, where it goes, and what you can do about it. If you have questions, email reggiebartleby@gmail.com.

What the game collects

InformationWhere it comes fromWhy
Your Discord user ID, username and display nameDiscord, when you allow the game's "identify" permissionTo show your name in races, keep you in your seat if you reconnect, and link your saved stats to you

The game does not ask for your email, phone number, real name, contacts, location, voice, or payment details. It does not read your messages or your voice chat. It does not use advertising or ad tracking.

Information kept on your device

The game saves some things in your browser's local storage so they are there next time: levels unlocked, in-game currency, personal bests, a cached copy of the friends board, and difficulty settings. This stays on your device. You can remove it by clearing site data for the game in your browser or Discord app.

Information kept between games

To keep your progress across games and devices, the game stores your Discord user ID, your name, and your game data (credits, wins, streaks and other race results) on a server. Other players may see your name and game results on leaderboards and friends' boards inside the game.

Who handles the data

The game uses these services to run. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Discord runs the Activity and provides your Discord identity. Discord Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare runs the race server and stores saved game data. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages hosts the game files and these pages. GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google (Google Sheets and Apps Script) may store game results and leaderboard data. Google Privacy Policy

I do not sell your information, rent it, or share it with advertisers. I only share it with the services above so the game can work, or if the law requires it.

How long it is kept

Race connection data (your ID and name while you are in a race) is held only while the race is running. Saved game data (your name, credits, wins, streaks and other race results) is kept so leaderboards and your progress carry over between games. It is deleted when any of these happens first:

  • you ask me to delete it (see "Your choices" below);
  • it is no longer needed to run the game;
  • the game stops operating or is shut down.

When the game shuts down, I will delete every copy of your Discord user ID, username, display name, and any game data linked to them, from every service listed above. The only thing I may keep is overall totals that can't be linked to any player (for example, "total races played"). These deletion rules follow Discord's Developer Terms of Service.

Your choices

  • See or delete your data: email reggiebartleby@gmail.com from any address and include your Discord username. I will send you what is stored about you, or delete it, within 30 days. If a request is complicated and I need more time, I will tell you within those 30 days, and I will finish within 2 more months at most.
  • Remove the game's access to your Discord account: in Discord, go to User Settings > Authorized Apps and remove Jump Duck Faster.
  • Clear local data: clear site data for the game in your browser or Discord app.

Children

The game is played through Discord, which requires users to be at least 13 and to meet the minimum age set by the laws of their country. The game is not meant for children below that age, and I do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, email me and I will delete their data.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS and secure WebSockets). Stored data is kept to what the game needs. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't put personal information in your Discord name that you wouldn't want other players to see.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the effective date above. If the change is significant, the game will show a notice.
