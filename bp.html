Project Fishnet · battle sample

The hex map won. Now try Echoes.

The hex map now has two new things to test: Friend Echoes and torchlight. Fight to fill the Echo meter, then call in a guildmate (or a wandering hero) for two actions. Spend both at once, or save one for next turn. The older square samples are still below for reference. The portraits and island map are early concept art; the little battle figures are still placeholders.

  • 1 Tap one of your crew
  • 2 Tap a lit space to move
  • 3 Tap a ghost marked red to attack

Palms, rocks and torches block movement. Ghosts float, so water never stops them. Try both grid settings too.
